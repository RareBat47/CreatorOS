# 02 - Talking-Head Template Specification

## 1. Layout Characteristics
- **Primary Visual**: User-uploaded A-roll video centered in vertical viewport.
- **Camera Dynamics**: Subtle slow zoom-in (scale 1.0 to 1.08 over 45s) to retain visual attention.
- **Captions**: Word-by-word kinetic highlight captions placed at 70% viewport height.
- **Overlay Elements**: Upper-third topic badges and lower-third brand watermark.
