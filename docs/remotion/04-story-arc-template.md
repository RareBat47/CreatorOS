# 04 - Story-Arc Template Specification

## 1. Layout Characteristics
- **Format**: Personal narrative or case-study breakdown.
- **Structure**:
  - Hook / Inciting Incident (0-3s): Dramatic opening statement.
  - The Struggle / Failure (3-15s): Darker background tint, slower pacing, zoom-in.
  - The Breakthrough / Epiphany (15-30s): High-contrast color flash, kinetic text burst.
  - The Lesson / Transformation (30-45s): Actionable rule and closing call to action.
