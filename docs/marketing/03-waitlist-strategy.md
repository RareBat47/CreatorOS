# 03 - Waitlist Strategy: CreatorOS

## 1. High-Converting Waitlist Mechanics
- **Headline**: "Turn one idea into a week of consistent, platform-ready content."
- **Interactive Teaser**: Allow visitors to type a single topic and preview 3 instant Model Council hooks.
- **Viral Referral Loop**: Users skip the waitlist queue by referring 3 fellow creators or sharing on X/LinkedIn.
- **VIP Onboarding**: Top 100 referrers receive free 1-on-1 Creator Brain calibration sessions with the founders.
