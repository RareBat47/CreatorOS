# 02 - Launch Content System: CreatorOS

## 1. Content Pillars for CreatorOS Brand
1. **Creator Pain**: Highlighting burnout, ideation block, and the horrors of manual editing.
2. **Product Demonstrations**: 30-second screen captures showing one idea turning into a fully rendered Remotion video.
3. **Contrarian Takes**: Why AI script wrappers produce garbage, and why Creator Memory is the only moat.
4. **Behind the Code**: Explaining code-based Remotion video and deterministic rendering.
5. **Customer Proof & Metrics**: Real retention curves and subscriber growth breakdowns.

## 2. Weekly Publishing Cadence
- **Monday**: Contrarian thought-leadership post (LinkedIn + X).
- **Tuesday**: Product demo video (Shorts + TikTok + Reels).
- **Wednesday**: Case study or retention breakdown.
- **Thursday**: Educational framework video.
- **Friday**: Weekly recap and behind-the-scenes progress video.
