# 01 - Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: CreatorOS

## 1. Multi-Phase GTM Strategy

### Phase 1: Build in Public (Days 1–30)
- Document the creation of CreatorOS on X and LinkedIn.
- Share raw Remotion code snippets and Model Council evaluation runs.
- Drive signups to a high-converting waitlist page.

### Phase 2: Founder-Led Content Engine (Days 31–60)
- Dogfood CreatorOS: All promotional videos for the product are produced using CreatorOS itself.
- Publish 5 short videos per week on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.
- Repurpose every video into an X thread and LinkedIn breakdown.

### Phase 3: High-Leverage Creator Partnerships (Days 61–90)
- Partner with 10 influential business coaches and founders.
- Onboard their brand into a custom Creator Brain and publish co-branded case studies demonstrating 10x throughput increases.
