# 01 - Frontend Design System: CreatorOS

## 1. Design Tokens & Aesthetic
CreatorOS features an obsidian dark-mode aesthetic tailored for high-focus creative operators.
- **Background**: Zinc-950 (`#09090b`) and Zinc-900 (`#18181b`).
- **Surface**: Zinc-800 borders (`#27272a`).
- **Accent**: Emerald-500 (`#10b981`) and Amber-500 (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography**: Inter (UI body) and Geist Sans / JetBrains Mono (code/timings).
