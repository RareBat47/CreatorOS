# 02 - Customer Personas: CreatorOS

## 1. Persona 1: The B2B Founder / Technical Operator (Primary Wedge)
- **Role**: Founder, Solo Bootstrapper, or Technical Co-founder in B2B SaaS / Services.
- **Goal**: Build organic authority on LinkedIn, Twitter, and YouTube Shorts to drive top-of-funnel pipeline for their company without spending 20 hours a week on content.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Understands their domain deeply but struggles to translate technical insights into engaging video hooks.
  - Embarrassed by robotic, generic AI content that degrades their professional credibility.
  - Hates complex video editing suites like Premiere Pro or Final Cut.
- **Willingness to Pay**: High ($79 - $199/month) if it directly replaces a $3,000/month freelance content repurposer.

## 2. Persona 2: The Executive / High-Ticket Coach
- **Role**: Business Coach, Fractional CMO, or Leadership Consultant.
- **Goal**: Consistently attract high-ticket clients by showcasing client success stories, contrarian frameworks, and actionable business breakdowns.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Inconsistent publishing schedule due to heavy client delivery workloads.
  - Needs strict brand guardrails to protect their reputation and high price point.
  - Has hundreds of hours of raw podcast/call footage but no time to turn it into short clips.
- **Willingness to Pay**: High ($79 - $299/month).

## 3. Persona 3: The Boutique Content Agency
- **Role**: Agency Owner or Creative Director managing 5-15 client brands.
- **Goal**: Multiply their team's throughput by 10x without hiring additional scriptwriters or junior video editors.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Context switching between wildly different client voices leads to brand dilution.
  - Manual video rendering and revision cycles take days per client.
- **Willingness to Pay**: Very High ($199 - $999/month for multi-creator brain support).
