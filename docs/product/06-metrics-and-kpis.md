# 06 - Metrics & KPIs: CreatorOS

## 1. North Star Metric
> **Weekly Videos Published (WVP)**: The number of unique videos generated in CreatorOS that are successfully rendered and marked as exported/published by active users each week.

If users publish weekly, their content engine is delivering tangible value, churn drops to near-zero, and word-of-mouth referral expands exponentially.

## 2. Product KPIs

### 2.1 Activation & Time-to-Value (TTV)
- **Signup-to-First-Render Time**: Target < 5 minutes for new users.
- **Onboarding Completion Rate**: Target > 75% of signups completing Creator Brain setup.
- **Hook Acceptance Rate**: Percentage of users who accept one of the first 3 generated hooks without requesting complete regeneration (Target > 80%).

### 2.2 Retention & Engagement
- **Week 4 User Retention**: Target > 45%.
- **Average Videos Created per Active User**: Target > 4 videos/month on Creator, > 10 videos/month on Pro.
- **Repurposing Asset Utilization**: Percentage of users who copy or export the secondary text assets (Target > 60%).

### 2.3 Quality & Model Evals
- **Model Council Rejection Rate**: Target < 5% of scripts requiring more than 2 revision passes.
- **Render Error Rate**: Target < 0.5% of BullMQ video rendering jobs failing.
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Target > 55 among users who have published 5+ videos.
