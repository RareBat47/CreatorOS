# 05 - Pricing & Business Model: CreatorOS

## 1. Unit Economics & Pricing Tiers

### 1.1 Free Trial (Product-Led Growth)
- **Price**: $0 (7-day trial or 3 free video renders with watermark).
- **Includes**: 1 Creator Brain, 3 Scene Spec generations, standard rendering queue.
- **Conversion Goal**: Upgrade to Creator plan within 7 days.

### 1.2 Creator Plan ($29/month or $290/year)
- **Target**: Individual solo creators and emerging personal brands.
- **Includes**:
  - 1 Active Creator Brain.
  - 15 Rendered Videos per month (1080p, no watermark).
  - Standard Model Council pipeline.
  - Basic Repurposing Package (Shorts captions + LinkedIn posts).
  - Email support.

### 1.3 Pro Plan ($79/month or $790/year)
- **Target**: Serious business operators, coaches, and active founders.
- **Includes**:
  - 3 Creator Brains (manage personal + company brand).
  - 45 Rendered Videos per month.
  - Priority Remotion rendering queue.
  - Full Omni-Channel Repurposing (Video, X Thread, LinkedIn, Newsletter).
  - Historical Retention & Performance Memory analytics.
  - Custom font and asset uploads.

### 1.4 Agency & Team Plan ($199 - $499/month)
- **Target**: Content agencies and venture studios.
- **Includes**:
  - 10+ Creator Brains.
  - Unlimited video rendering passes.
  - Dedicated rendering workers / custom templates.
  - Multi-seat collaboration and client review portal.
  - API and webhook access for programmatic batch creation.

## 2. Revenue Projections (Path to $1M ARR)
To reach $1,000,000 ARR (~$83,333 MRR):
- 600 Pro Customers ($79/mo) = $47,400 MRR
- 150 Agency Customers ($199/mo) = $29,850 MRR
- 250 Creator Customers ($29/mo) = $7,250 MRR
- **Total**: 1,000 paying accounts = **$84,500 MRR ($1.014M ARR)**.
